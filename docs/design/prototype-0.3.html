New in this prototype

  1. Talk while you point: turn on Inspect (pointer, or Alt), then hold Space, talk, and click the things you mean. The pointer glows while it listens. Let go to edit and send.
  2. Place: the image button opens a palette in the dock. Drag a picture anywhere on the page, or click one to drop it in view (pasting and files from your computer work too). Move it freely: it snaps to the edges and centers of what's around it. Resize from a corner, rotate with the round handle, align it in the dock. Remove it with Delete or the ×.
  3. A smaller dock: no Talk button, and Batch appears only once you've collected something.
