How it works

Three steps, on the page you're already looking at

No screenshots pasted into chat, no guessing at class names. The tool sits on your running app in development and talks to your agent over MCP.

01

Point at it

Press Alt and click any element: a button, a wavy line, a card you don't know the name of. Agent Hover finds the component and the exact line that renders it.

Alt then click
02

Say what to change

Type it or say it. Your agent gets a short brief with the file, the line, how the element looks now and what it must not touch.

Enter to Ask · ⌘↵ to send
03

Check it where it happened

The task stays on the element while the agent works. When it's done, compare before and after, keep it, undo it, or follow up.

K Keep · U Undo · F Follow up

What your agent receives

A short, exact brief instead of a vague description

  • WhereThe component, its file and the line that renders the element, even on React 19 and through shared UI blocks.
  • How it looks nowRendered size, font, colours, padding, radius and layout, measured from the page.
  • What not to touchChange this element only, follow the file's styling approach, and stay off locked components.
  • Just enough sourceAbout 90 lines around the element by default. Compact, Standard or Deep in Settings.
task.md~1.1k tokens
# Change request: <button> “Book a demo” in <Hero />

## What to do
Make this button larger with a subtle arrow icon

## Where
- Element: `button#hero-primary-cta` with text “Book a demo”
- Rendered by: `<Hero />` in `src/components/Hero.tsx`, lines 14–80
- In the source, the element is around line 55
- It is rendered through `<Button>` (line 54), a shared component:
  change this usage rather than `<Button>` itself.

## The element now
- 135×44px · font 14px/500 · color #ffffff on #17181a
  padding 0px 24px · radius full · flex row, gap 8px

## Constraints
- Change this element only; keep the rest of <Hero /> as it is.
- Follow the existing styling approach (Tailwind classes).

Local by design

Runs on your machine, in development only

Dev only

The plugin runs with vite dev and adds nothing to production builds.

Local bridge

It listens on 127.0.0.1, checks the page's origin and needs a per-session token.

No telemetry

The only outbound request is a daily update check to npm, with no project data. You can turn it off.
